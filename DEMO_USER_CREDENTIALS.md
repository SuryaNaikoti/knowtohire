## 💡 Access Instructions

### Option 1: Standard Login Form
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/login` in your web browser.
2. Enter any email and password from the matrix above.
3. Click **Login Profile** to access the corresponding dashboard.

### Option 2: Instant Simulator Role Switcher (No Password Required)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/dashboard/candidate` (or any dashboard view).
2. Locate the **"Simulator Switcher"** at the bottom of the left sidebar.
3. Switch between **`Role: Candidate`**, **`Role: Employer`**, or **`Role: Admin`** to test all 3 dashboard views immediately.
