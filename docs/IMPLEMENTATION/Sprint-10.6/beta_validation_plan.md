# Closed Beta Validation Plan

Validating end-to-end features with real candidates and employers.

---

## 1. Beta Cohorts
- **Candidates**: 50 active frontend and backend engineers.
- **Employers**: 5 pilot recruitment teams.

---

## 2. Journey Auditing
- **Candidate Journey**: Check resume parsing accuracy, roadmap generation usability, and goals checklist interaction.
- **Employer Journey**: Validate candidate shortlists, pipeline changes, and interview scheduling workflows.

---

## 3. Feedback Loop & Support
- Embedded feedback form widgets in candidate sidebar.
- Standardized bug reporting schemas sent directly to support channels.
