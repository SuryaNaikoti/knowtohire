# Performance Optimization Plan

Measurable targets for dashboard and parser performance.

---

## 1. Targets & Metrics
- **Initial Page Load**: Under 2.0 seconds on standard 3G connections.
- **Dashboard Render Time**: Under 300 milliseconds.
- **Resume Analysis Pipeline**: Under 1.5 seconds.
- **Job Matching Engine**: Under 100 milliseconds.

---

## 2. Optimization Protocols
- Maintain dynamic lazy loading for dashboard widgets.
- Memoize candidate adapter mapping results.
